**Document type:** Business plan, product requirements and technical
execution blueprint\
**Initial market:** India, starting with Gujarat\
**Primary niche:** Premium digital wedding invitations and interactive

# 1. Executive Summary

## 1.1 Business concept

Build a premium digital invitation and wedding experience studio that
creates visually distinctive, interactive and personalized invitation
websites. Customers receive a personalized URL that they can share with
family and friends through WhatsApp, Instagram and other channels.

The product should feel like a digital experience---not an ordinary
website or static PDF invitation.

Reference inspiration: - https://www.scrollvite.com/preview/5 -
https://visualaurastudio.com/services/embroidered-rose-theme

Use these websites as market and experience references only. Do not copy
their source code, layouts, illustrations, artwork, animations or
branding.

## 1.2 Vision

Become a recognized digital invitation studio in India, known for: -
Distinctive and premium invitation designs. - Smooth animations and
interactive experiences. - Personalized wedding themes. - Mobile-first
invitation websites. - RSVP and guest-management features. - Reliable
delivery and customer service. - A scalable production process built on
reusable technology.

## 1.3 Positioning

**Business category:** Digital Invitation & Wedding Experience Studio.

**Positioning statement:** We create personalized digital invitations
and interactive wedding experiences that make every celebration
memorable.

**Suggested tagline:** "Beyond Invitations, Into Experiences."

The brand name is not final. Other naming directions include Vowora,
InviteAura, Wedora, VowVibe, EverVow, WedStory, InviteNest, Celebrio,
VowStudio and ShaadiVerse. Check domain availability, social handles and
trademark conflicts before selection.

**Price: ₹799 one-time payment**\

### Customer journey

1.  Customer purchases Signature Digital Invite.
2.  Customer submits wedding details and contact information.
3.  Customer shares wedding colors or design inspiration.
4.  Customer uploads up to 30 photographs and any approved assets.
5.  Team confirms that all required content has been received.
6.  Design and development begin.
7.  Customer receives a preview link.
8.  Customer reviews and approves the invitation.
9.  Final invitation is published.
10. Customer shares the personalized URL with guests.

### Delivery and scope conditions

-   The 48-hour delivery clock begins only after payment and receipt of
    all required content.
-   One invitation website is included per purchase.
-   Maximum 30 photographs.
-   One primary color palette.
-   One standard RSVP form and one Google Sheets integration.
-   Minor text, date, venue and content corrections are included until
    the wedding day.
-   Major redesigns, new sections, substantial layout changes and
    additional functionality are quoted separately.
-   Custom domain purchase is not included; the included URL uses the
    studio's subdomain.
-   Hosting is included for one year from publication. Renewal terms and
    price must be disclosed.
-   Customer must provide music they have rights to use or choose from
    an approved licensed library.
-   Autoplay cannot be guaranteed because browsers commonly restrict
    audio autoplay; provide a clear play/unmute control.
-   Customer must provide accurate event and venue information.
-   Define a reasonable response/approval window so customer delays do
    not become a delivery guarantee breach.

### Design principles

Each Signature Digital Invite must have a distinct visual presentation.
Do not merely select a finished template and replace names, photographs
and colors.

Possible design directions: - Floral illustrations. - Royal Indian
wedding aesthetics. - Minimalist luxury. - Traditional Gujarati
aesthetics. - Embroidery- and handcrafted-inspired visuals. - Romantic
pastel themes. - Modern editorial layouts. - Custom typography and
opening animations.

The underlying code, layout primitives and functional components may be
reused. The final visual composition and styling must be created
specifically for each customer.

**Headline:** Your Wedding. Your Colors. Your Signature Invitation.

**Description:** A completely personalized digital wedding invitation,
designed exclusively for you. No ready-made finished templates and no
ordinary invitation designs---just a beautiful, interactive wedding
website that reflects your story, your colors and your celebration.

**Offer:** ₹799 one-time \| Delivered within 48 hours after all content

## 12.3 Theme engine

Do not create a separate application for every invitation.

Use a shared rendering engine:

**Invitation Data + Theme Configuration + Reusable Sections + Custom
Visual Assets = Published Invitation**

Example configuration: - Theme: Embroidered Rose. - Background: Ivory. -
Primary color: Dusty rose. - Secondary color: Champagne gold. -
Typography: Elegant serif. - Opening: Floral envelope. - Animation:
Embroidery-inspired reveal. - Sections: Cover, Couple, Events, Gallery
and RSVP.

A customer-specific design can use the same functional components with a
distinct visual composition. Bespoke projects may include custom
components.

For Signature Digital Invite, do not simply assign a finished theme and
replace its text. Create a customer-specific visual design while reusing
the underlying technical building blocks.

# 20. Strategic Approach: Service First, Platform Later

Do not begin by building a large self-service SaaS platform. Start with
a managed studio and validate demand.

## Stage A -- Done-for-you studio

Customers choose a package or design direction. The studio creates and
publishes the invitation.

Validate: - Demand. - Preferred designs. - Pricing. - Sales channels. -
Production time. - Profitability.

## Stage B -- Semi-automated platform

Introduce: - Admin-driven invitation creation. - Theme configuration. -
Reusable components. - Automated publishing. - Payment integration. -
Preview links. - Delivery and hosting tracking.

## Stage C -- Self-service platform

Eventually allow customers to: - Register. - Select a design
direction. - Enter wedding information. - Upload photographs. -
Customize permitted options. - Preview invitations. - Purchase
packages. - Publish websites.

## Stage D -- B2B and white-label

Allow wedding planners and agencies to: - Create customer orders. -
Select designs. - Manage invitations. - Operate under their own brand. -
Purchase bulk packages.

This staged approach reduces initial investment and avoids building
features before demand is validated.
